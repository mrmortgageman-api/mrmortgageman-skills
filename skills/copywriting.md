Before writing a single word, read product-marketing-context.md.

Do not write without loading that context first.

## Writing Framework

Every piece of MrMortgageMan copy follows this structure regardless of format or length.

Step 1: Audience first.
Confirm who this is for before writing anything.
- First-time buyer: reassuring, simple, one clear next step
- Move-up buyer: strategic, equity-focused, make the math visible
- Realtor partner: fast, confident, deal-outcome focused
- Professional referral partner: analytical, peer-to-peer, client-outcome focused

Step 2: Lead with their problem, not your product.
Never open with who Scott is or what company he works for.
Open with what the reader is experiencing or worried about.

Step 3: Diagnose, educate, lead.
Diagnose like a physician. Educate like a coach. Lead like a captain.
Apply all three in every piece of copy.

Step 4: One clear outcome.
Every page drives toward one outcome. Define it before writing. Do not drift.

Step 5: One CTA.
One action. Easy to take. Specific.

---

## Hard Rules - Never Break

- No em dashes. Period or comma.
- No banned words: hopefully, maybe, no problem, best rate, fingers crossed, crushing it, killing it, game-changer, revolutionary, act now, limited time, dont miss out
- Every sentence under 20 words.
- Lead with confidence, not pressure.
- No jargon on first read.
- No rate quotes in evergreen copy.
- Do not open with Scotts name or title.
- You/your dominates. I/we is secondary.

## Page Copy Frameworks

Homepage:

Hero (above the fold):
- Headline: their problem or desire in 8 words or fewer
- Subhead: what Scott does differently in one sentence
- CTA: book the ILC

Headline formula: [Outcome they want] without [fear they have]
Example: "A mortgage strategy. Not just a rate quote."

Problem section:
- Acknowledge what most lenders do wrong
- 2-3 short paragraphs
- Make the reader feel understood

Solution section:
- Introduce the 5-Pillar Mortgage Advisory System by name
- One paragraph per pillar, plain language
- No jargon

Social proof section:
- Client outcome statements, not star reviews
- Format: [Situation] -> [Outcome Scott delivered]

CTA section:
When the timing is right, here is where to start.

---

Realtor Partner Page:

Hero:
- Headline: what agents actually need from a lender
- Lead with deal outcomes: speed, clean approvals, listing agents trust
- CTA: Let us talk about your next buyer

Differentiators (3 max):
1. Pre-approvals listing agents actually trust
2. Scenario modeling before the offer is written
3. Communication cadence that keeps deals calm

How it works:
- 4-hour pre-approval turnaround
- Buyer stress-tested before writing offers
- Scott is the deal quarterback during escrow

---

Buyer Landing Page (SignalStrike B2C):

Hero:
- Speak directly to their emotion: excited but scared
- Do not pitch. Acknowledge.
- CTA: book the ILC

What they will know after 30 minutes:
- Exactly what they qualify for
- Payment at different price points
- How much cash they need after closing
- Which loan structure makes sense

What makes this different:
- Not a rate quote. A financial model.
- Built before house hunting, not after.

---

LinkedIn Profile / Bio:

Headline (220 char max):
Job title + core value proposition in plain language. No jargon.

Example: "Senior Loan Consultant | New American Funding | I build the financial strategy behind your mortgage before you write an offer"

About section:
- Open with the readers situation, not Scotts credentials
- Introduce the 5-Pillar system by name
- One paragraph on what working with Scott looks like in practice
- Close with one CTA

Never include: years of experience as opener, passionate about helping people, lists of loan products, rate language.

---

Google Business Profile:

Business description (750 char max):
- Open with positioning, not product
- Include search terms naturally: mortgage, home loan, pre-approval, Walnut Creek, East Bay, Northern California
- Close with one CTA

Posts: educational, not promotional. One insight per post. 150-300 words. No rate quotes.

---

Facebook MrMortgageMan Persona:

Voice: value through thoughtful responses. Not pitching. Not overposting.
Format: conversational. Short paragraphs. No bullet points in posts.
Frequency: quality over volume. Never post more than once per day.
Engagement: open-ended questions that invite response.

Post formula:
- Observation about what buyers or agents are experiencing
- One insight that adds clarity
- Open question or soft CTA
