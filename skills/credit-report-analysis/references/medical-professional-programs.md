## Redwood Sequoia — Medical Professionals Program

Primary Residence | Purchase, Rate & Term Refinance | 1-unit only | Manual underwrite required
(no AUS)

| FICO | Max LTV | Max Loan Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 680 | 95% | $2,000,000 |
| 680 | 100% | $1,500,000 |
| 720 | 100% | $2,000,000 |

- Minimum LTV required is 90.01% (this program is built for high-LTV scenarios, not low-LTV)
- DTI: 45% if LTV > 95% or ARM/15-yr; 50% if LTV ≤ 95%
- Loan amount range: $100,000–$2,000,000 (ARM minimum $350,000)
- Eligible borrowers include CRNA (with DNAP or DNP), MD, DO, DDS, DMD, PharmD, DVM/VMD, DPM,
  and medical residents/fellows/interns holding one of these degrees
- **As of 05/20/2026: HPML allowed on a pre-close exception basis only** — loan must be fully
  underwritten before requesting the exception from underwriting. Without an approved
  exception, HPML loans remain ineligible on this program.
- Derogatory credit seasoning: bankruptcy 4 yrs, foreclosure 4 yrs, short sale/DIL 4 yrs,
  notice of default 4 yrs. Events seasoned 10+ years don't need to be considered.
- **Medical collections may remain outstanding if under $10,000 in aggregate** — relevant
  detail for this specific borrower population, worth remembering when reviewing a medical
  professional's report.
- Non-medical judgments/tax liens/charge-offs/past-due accounts must be satisfied or brought
  current at or before closing.
- Credit inquiries within the most recent 90 days require confirmation no new undisclosed
  debt was opened.

## Silver Hill Capital — High LTV Medical Professionals

Primary Residence | Purchase, Rate & Term Refinance | 30-yr fixed & ARMs

| FICO | Max LTV/CLTV/HCLTV | Max Loan Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 700 | 100%¹ | $1,000,000 |
| 720 | 100%¹ | $1,500,000 |
| 700 | 95% | $2,000,000 |

¹ Non-Permanent Resident Aliens capped at 95% LTV/CLTV/HCLTV regardless of score

- Must be Safe Harbor QM — HPML and HPCT loans are NOT permitted on this program (contrast
  with Redwood, which now allows HPML via exception)
- Cash-out not permitted; rate/term refi and purchase only
- Derogatory seasoning differs from Redwood: bankruptcy 4 yrs, **foreclosure 7 yrs** (longer
  than Redwood's 4), short sale/DIL 4 yrs
- No Single Loan Variances (SLVs) granted on this product — no exception path

## Quorum — Medical/Doctor Loan Program (part of Quorum's broader Portfolio program)

Doctor Loan Eligibility Matrix | Primary Residence only | Practicing MD, DO, DPM, DDS, DMD
only (does not include CRNA, PharmD, DVM, or residents/fellows — narrower borrower list than
Redwood or Silver Hill)

| Purpose | Max LTV | Max CLTV | Max 1st Mortgage | Max 2nd Mortgage | Min FICO | Reserves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase/Rate-Term | 80% | 100% | $1,000,000 | $500,000 | 680 | 6 mo PITIA |
| Cash-Out Refi | 75% | 100% | $1,000,000 | $500,000 | 680 | 12 mo PITIA |

- No minimum borrower contribution required; gift funds acceptable
- Subordinate/2nd mortgage financing must be secured through Quorum itself
- Future employment start date: up to 9 months between closing and start date with signed
  offer letter/contract
- **This program's minimum FICO floor (680) is a hard cutoff, not a tiered scale like Redwood
  or Silver Hill** — below 680, Quorum's doctor program isn't an option regardless of LTV

## Quick cross-program comparison at common score points

| Score | Redwood Sequoia | Silver Hill | Quorum Doctor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 680 | Eligible — up to $2M at 95% LTV or $1.5M at 100% LTV | Not eligible (floor is 700) | Eligible — $1M at 80% LTV |
| 700 | Eligible (same as 680 tier) | Eligible — $1M at 100% LTV or $2M at 95% LTV | Eligible |
| 720 | Eligible — up to $2M at 100% LTV | Eligible — $1.5M at 100% LTV | Eligible |

**Practical read for tier-crossing conversations:** 700 is the more consequential threshold
for Silver Hill specifically — below it, that program isn't available at all, regardless of
LTV. Redwood's story is different: 680 already opens meaningful LTV/loan-amount combinations,
and 720 is where it improves further. Which program matters most depends on the borrower's
LTV target and eligible-degree status, not just their score — check eligible borrower lists
above before assuming a program applies.
