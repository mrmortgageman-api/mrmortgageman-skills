Secondary:

## SIGNAL CATEGORIES

Use one primary signal category per person.

1. First-Time Buyer Signal
Person asks about buying their first home, down payment, credit, affordability, rent vs buy, pre-approval, or where to start.

2. Payment Anxiety Signal
Person mentions high rates, monthly payment concerns, affordability, feeling priced out, waiting for rates to drop, or uncertainty about whether buying still makes sense.

3. Move-Up / Life Change Signal
Person mentions growing family, job change, school change, relocation, needing more space, downsizing, marriage, divorce, inheritance, or other life transition that may connect to housing.

4. Credit / Qualification Signal
Person asks about credit score, income, debt, self-employment, student loans, collections, bankruptcy, or whether they can qualify.

5. Home Equity / HELOC Signal
Person asks about remodels, ADUs, debt consolidation, home repairs, contractor costs, equity, second mortgage, HELOC, or tapping home value.

6. Refinance / Rate Watch Signal
Person mentions current mortgage rate, refinance question, wanting lower payment, escrow shortage, mortgage insurance, cash-out refi, or whether refinancing makes sense.

7. Investor / Rental Signal
Person asks about buying a rental, DSCR, investment property, house hacking, moving out and keeping current home, or rental cash flow.

8. Realtor Needed / Search Readiness Signal
Person asks where to buy, what neighborhoods make sense, how to start looking, whether they need a Realtor, or how to compare areas.

9. Past Client Opportunity Signal
Past client mentions home improvement, growing family, equity, financial planning, relocation, refinance, investment property, or someone close to them buying.

10. Engagement With Scott Signal
Person liked, commented, replied, shared, DM'd, or reacted to Scott's recent mortgage, housing, or personal-brand content in a way that creates a natural opening.

## SCORING

Rank each opportunity using a simple 10-point score.

Score factors:
- Recency: today or last 72 hours
- Intent strength: direct mortgage, housing, payment, equity, or moving signal
- Relationship: past client, known contact, warm social connection, referral source, or engaged follower
- Actionability: Scott can make a useful move in under 2 minutes
- Sensitivity: outreach can be done naturally without sounding invasive
- Timing: reaching out today would feel helpful, not opportunistic

SCORING GUIDANCE:
9–10: Clear mortgage or housing intent, recent, actionable, warm or known relationship.
7–8: Strong housing or life-event signal, but may need softer educational touch.
5–6: Possible signal but indirect. Include only if no stronger signals exist.
Below 5: Do not include.

## RULES

EVIDENCE RULE:
Evidence must be a 1–2 sentence summary with a direct quote when available.
If no direct quote is available, clearly say "summary only."

PRIVACY RULE:
Do not expose unnecessary personal details.
Do not overstate sensitive life events.
Do not write outreach that sounds like Scott is monitoring them.
Use the public signal as context, not as surveillance.

ACTION RULE:
Suggested Action must be executable by Scott in under 2 minutes.

OUTREACH RULE:
Exact Outreach Line must sound like Scott:
- Calm
- Useful
- Human
- No hype
- No urgency pressure
- No "checking in"
- No generic mortgage pitch
- No fear-based language
- No "I saw you're going through..." on sensitive life events

Good examples:

First-time buyer:
"Hey [Name], saw the homebuying question. The first step is usually not house hunting, it's getting clear on payment and cash to close. Happy to help you frame that out if useful."

Payment anxiety:
"Hey [Name], saw your comment about payment being the hard part right now. That's exactly where most buyers are stuck. I can send you a simple payment breakdown if it would help."

HELOC / renovation:
"Hey [Name], saw the remodel question. Before you start comparing financing options, it usually helps to know what your home equity can realistically support. I can help you sanity-check that."

Past client:
"Hey [Name], saw your home project post. Hope you guys are doing great. If you want to understand what your equity looks like before making decisions, I'm happy to help."

Bad examples:
"Rates are changing fast, call me today."
"You need to get pre-approved immediately."
"I saw you might be getting divorced and need mortgage help."
"You're probably sitting on a ton of equity."
"Let's get you into my funnel."

TIEBREAKER:
If two people have similar signal strength, prioritize:
1. Past client or known relationship
2. Warm engagement with Scott
3. Direct mortgage or housing question
4. Local NorCal relevance
5. Fastest useful action
6. Lowest sensitivity risk

DO NOT INCLUDE:
- Consumers with no clear signal
- Cold strangers with weak intent
- Private or sensitive assumptions
- Medical, legal, or family crisis details unless the person made them public and housing-relevant
- Generic market summaries
- More than five people
- B2B agent leads
- Tasks that require more than 2 minutes
- Outreach that feels opportunistic or invasive

OUTPUT QUALITY CHECK:
Before finalizing, ask:
1. Is the signal real and recent?
2. Is the outreach helpful?
3. Would Scott be comfortable sending this exact message?
4. Does this protect trust?
5. Is this a 2-minute action?
