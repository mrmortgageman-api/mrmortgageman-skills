## Formation Philosophy

Most operators run one giant AI lead scanner. That creates the equivalent of 10 players all chasing the ball. Everything becomes noise. Nothing gets executed.

SignalStrike runs two separate formations with distinct objectives, tempo, and output.

| Formation | Engine | Style of Play | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| SignalStrike B2B | Agent Partnership Engine | Possession football | Build and deepen referral relationships |
| SignalStrike B2C | Consumer Lead Engine | Counterattack | Capture immediate opportunity and life events |

These two lanes are not merged. Possession build-up and transition attacks are different phases of play. Keeping them separate is what makes SignalStrike scalable.

---

## SignalStrike B2B — Agent Partnership Engine

**Goal:** Control distribution. One great agent relationship can feed dozens of transactions.

**Primary Signals:**
- Lending questions from agents
- Listing pressure (price drops, stale inventory, reduced traffic)
- Production momentum (3+ closings in 30 days)
- Engagement with Scott's content
- Friction signals (timeline, appraisal, lender, affordability pain)
- Market commentary
- Referral moment opportunities

**Outcome:** Distribution. Referral pipeline depth.

---

## SignalStrike B2C — Consumer Lead Engine

**Goal:** Identify immediate opportunity. Solve problems. Create conversations.

**Primary Signals:**
- First-time buyer questions
- Payment anxiety
- Move-up and life change signals
- Credit and qualification questions
- HELOC and home equity opportunities
- Refinance and rate-watch signals
- Investor and rental questions
- Search readiness
- Past client opportunities
- Engagement with Scott's content

**Outcome:** Direct revenue. Shorter-lived opportunities requiring faster action.
