## MONITOR SOURCES

Scan only sources that are accessible and permitted.

Primary:
- Facebook agent profiles, agent posts, comments, local real estate groups
- Instagram agent posts, stories, comments, engagement
- LinkedIn agent posts, activity, job changes, listing announcements
- Reddit real estate discussions where relevant

Secondary:
- Public listing activity
- Public price reductions
- Public closing announcements
- Public market commentary
- Scott's recent social post engagement

Do not invent signals.
Do not infer private facts.
Do not report anything you cannot support with visible evidence.

## SIGNAL CATEGORIES

Use one primary signal category per agent.

1. Lending Question Signal
Agent asked about rates, pre-approval, financing, buyer payment, credit, underwriting, docs, or loan timing.

2. Listing Pressure Signal
Agent has a price drop, stale listing, reduced buyer traffic, listing sitting longer than expected, or market-shift concern.

3. Production Momentum Signal
Agent appears to have closed 3+ deals in the last 30 days or is visibly active with buyer/seller transactions.

4. Scott Engagement Signal
Agent liked, commented, replied, shared, DM'd, or meaningfully engaged with Scott's recent content.

5. Friction Signal
Agent mentioned pain around rates, timelines, documentation, lender communication, appraisal, insurance, buyer affordability, or deal uncertainty.

6. Market Commentary Signal
Agent posted commentary about rates, inventory, buyer demand, affordability, market shift, price reductions, or local activity.

7. Referral Moment Signal
Agent is in a moment where a warm, useful mortgage perspective could help them serve a buyer, seller, or audience today.

---

## SCORING

Rank each opportunity using a simple 10-point score.

Score factors:
- Recency: today or last 72 hours
- Relevance: directly connected to mortgage, buyer, listing, or market pressure
- Relationship: known agent, past engagement, target account, or strategic partner
- Actionability: Scott can take a useful step in under 2 minutes
- Timing: outreach today would feel natural, not forced

## RULES

EVIDENCE RULE:
Evidence must be a 1–2 sentence summary with a direct quote when available. If no direct quote is available, clearly say "summary only."

ACTION RULE:
Suggested Action must be executable by Scott in under 2 minutes.

OUTREACH RULE:
Exact Outreach Line must sound like Scott:
- Calm
- Useful
- Human
- No hype
- No hard sell
- No "checking in"
- No generic mortgage pitch

Good example:
"Hey [Name], saw your note about buyers pushing back on payment. I'm seeing the same thing. If helpful, I can send you a quick payment strategy breakdown you can use with clients this week."

Bad example:
"Rates are changing fast, let's talk before it's too late."

TIEBREAKER:
If two agents have similar signal strength, prioritize:
1. Existing relationship
2. Active buyer/listing pressure
3. Recent engagement with Scott
4. Agent with audience influence
5. Cleanest 2-minute action

DO NOT INCLUDE:
- Generic market summaries
- Agents with no clear signal
- Unsupported guesses
- Long explanations
- More than five agents
- B2C consumer leads
- Tasks that require more than 2 minutes
