# Strategic Communication Engine
## MrMortgageMan | Elite Negotiation + Positioning Framework

## VOSS FRAMEWORK

1. Tactical Empathy: Start with their position, not yours
2. Label the Concern: Name the underlying fear, not the surface complaint
3. Mirror: Repeat a key phrase. One mirror per exchange maximum.
4. Calibrated Questions: How / What / Walk me through. Never "Why."

## BEHAVIORAL FINANCE LEVERS

| Lever | When | Language |
|---|---|---|
| Loss Aversion | Rate shopper | "The risk most people try to avoid is a delayed close." |
| Decision Simplicity | Overwhelmed | "There's really one number that matters here." |
| Future Regret Avoidance | Stalling | "The decision most clients want to avoid is looking back..." |
| Control Bias | Resistant | "My job is to give you the full picture. You decide." |
